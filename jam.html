How scoring works

Each judge scores every Room. All three scores are averaged, with a maximum score of 19 points.

Game Design

0-5

Use objectives, enemies, collectibles, and puzzle mechanics to make the Room fun.

  1. 0 Totally unplayable. Players cannot move or do anything on entry.
  2. 1 Playable, but there is nothing to do.
  3. 2 Mechanics or objectives make it more than a space to navigate.
  4. 3 The objective makes sense and every required object is present and usable.
  5. 4 The objective is fun and challenging.
  6. 5 Judges want to keep replaying for faster leaderboard times.

Level Design

0-3

Consider the layout, interesting routes, and whether reachable areas work as expected.

  1. 0 Totally unplayable. Players cannot move or do anything on entry.
  2. 1 The Room can be played.
  3. 2 Every area that looks accessible can be reached.
  4. 3 The Room is interesting to explore and may flow well into neighboring spaces.

Neighbors blocking travel from their side will not count against you.

Level Decoration

0-4

Use decorative tiles and objects to make the Room feel beautiful and complete.

  1. 0 Bare, with no decorative tiles or objects.
  2. 1 Some decoration is present.
  3. 2 Decoration supports the Room's theme.
  4. 3 Detailed, polished, and complete.

+1 bonus Lighting and weather enhance the mood.

Music

0-4

Use the music editor to create a mood for your Room.

  1. 0 The music is painful to listen to.
  2. 1 There is no music.
  3. 2 The music is listenable.
  4. 3 The music is catchy enough to hum after playing.

+1 bonus Multiple sequencer tracks create a longer song.

Storytelling

0-3

Add a narrative told through level design and text.

  1. 0 No story seems to be present.
  2. 1 Basic environmental storytelling.
  3. 2 Signs and NPCs convey more of the story.
  4. 3 The single-Room story hooks the judges and leaves them wanting more.

Entry and conduct rules

  • The Room must be a newly claimed space between July 20 and July 26.
  • Submit your username, connected email, and Room coordinates or URL by July 26 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time (NYC).
  • Make sure the coordinates match the Room URL so you do not submit the wrong Room.
  • Keep a valid email connected to your WAMP account so the team can contact you.
  • Custom objects and backgrounds are allowed when your Build Level permits them, but they do not earn bonus points.
  • Prizes may be paid in USD through PayPal or in USDC to an Ethereum wallet address.
  • Judges will play experiences live on stream on Tuesday the 28th and record scores. Final results will be announced after every submission has been played and scored.
  • Judges' decisions are final. Harassment, score ranting, or other unsportsmanlike conduct can result in removal from events, WAMP, Discord, and related social spaces.
