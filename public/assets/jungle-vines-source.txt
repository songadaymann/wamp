Jungle Vines asset credits
==========================

LunarDrift Studios
Source: https://lunadrift-studios.itch.io/jungle-vines-background-2d-pixel-pack
Used for: the six-layer Jungle Vines parallax background, the Jungle Vines
decorative tileset, and the seven small vine-sprig objects.
License note from the source page: personal and commercial use and modification
are allowed; credit and a link back to the source page are required.

Pixelina
Source: https://pixelina-art.itch.io/vines-asset-pack-by-pixelina
Used for: the six climbable vine objects, Hanging Jungle Creepers, and Looping
Jungle Vine. The climbable objects are lossless vertical compositions of the
pack's individual top, middle, and (where supplied) bottom segments.
License note from the source page: CC0; credit is appreciated but not required.

The Pixelina header banner and combined reference sheet are not runtime assets,
because they duplicate the individual source sprites. Every unique gameplay
sprite supplied in the pack is represented by the objects above.
