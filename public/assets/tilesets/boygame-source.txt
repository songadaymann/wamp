Boygame tileset asset provenance

Source: user-supplied local `Gameboy assets` pack.

Imported on 2026-08-13 on the native 16 x 16 game grid.
Terrain is stored as an 8 x 7 atlas. Chests, signs, decorations, coins, and the
heart were split into standalone Boygame objects; the supplied multi-frame coin,
heart, and wall-torch artwork remains animated.
The Player_notmove.png, Player_jump.png, and Player_dead.png sheets were intentionally excluded.
